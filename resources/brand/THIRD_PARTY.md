# Application marks

The WhatsApp and Signal glyphs in `src/renderer/public` are sourced from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0), with white fill for use on the corresponding brand color.

- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/whatsapp.svg
- https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/signal.svg

WhatsApp and Signal names and marks belong to their respective owners. Their use identifies the application being opened and does not imply endorsement of HelloDog.
